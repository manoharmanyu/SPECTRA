GOVERNANCE & ETHICS

About SPECTRA Platform

Mission, methodology, limitations & responsible use

Dedicated to upholding the truth of human communication and defending democratic discourse.

Our Mission

In an era where synthetic media can counterfeit human identity with cinematic realism, SPECTRA exists to provide an uncompromised scientific benchmark for media truth. We empower journalists, forensic examiners, legal institutions, and cybersecurity teams to detect deception before it spreads.

Methodology

SPECTRA prioritizes physics-based and biological invariants over purely generative neural classifiers. While deepfake generators evolve rapidly, physical camera optics, optical sensor noise, and cardiovascular blood volume dynamics cannot be easily forged without revealing mathematical anomalies.

Known Limitations

Forensic certainty is probabilistic. Highly compressed social media video, extreme resolution reduction (below 240p), and deliberate adversarial adversarial noise injections can lower confidence margins. SPECTRA provides transparent confidence bounds and never presents probabilistic scores as absolute certainty.

Responsible Use & Privacy

SPECTRA operates under a strict Zero-Knowledge Biometric policy. Uploaded media is analyzed in volatile isolated memory runtimes and automatically erased upon session conclusion. Our tools are prohibited from mass biometric surveillance or non-consensual tracking.
